# Poetry audio

The firmware ships 16 kHz mono signed 16-bit PCM generated by
`tools/build_poetry_audio.py`. There are no third-party sample packs in this
application.

## Poem narration

The three narration clips under `assets/audio/narration/` were spoken with the
Windows zh-CN system voice (Microsoft Huihui) through System.Speech, at a slow
rate suitable for a seven-year-old, then resampled to 16 kHz mono and trimmed.
The recordings are committed so the firmware build is reproducible offline.
The voice model itself is not redistributed.

| Poem id | Title | Source text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | *Chunxiao* (Spring Dawn) | Title, author, and all four lines |
| 2 | *Jingye Si* (Quiet Night Thoughts) | Title, author, and all four lines |
| 16 | *Deng Guanque Lou* (Climbing Stork Tower) | Title, author, and all four lines |

## Background music

The 12.8-second loop is synthesised from a C-major pentatonic pluck, a slow
low pad, and a sparse high shimmer. It is generated entirely in
`tools/build_poetry_audio.py`; no external recording is used.

## Interaction cues

Five gentle cues (`move`, `confirm`, `correct`, `wrong`, `unlock`) are
synthesised sine/triangle envelopes in the same script. They deliberately avoid
the arcade-style beams and growls of the math matching game.
